SALAD ESCAPE

Maria is making minestrone di verdure… and you're the missing vegetable.

🥬 How to escape

You're a little salad in Maria's flat. She's vegan and she's cooking. Don't end up in the pot.

  1. Find 3 chopsticks on the floor. They build a ramp up to the sofa.
  2. Find 3 basil leaves on the sofa. Your herb friends give you Super Leap.
  3. Jump onto the sofa back, get to the window sill, hold E to open the window and jump out!
WASD / ArrowsMove (relative to the camera) MouseLook around (click the game to lock the mouse) SpaceJump ShiftSprint (uses stamina, and Maria can hear you) EInteract EscPause

🫥 Hide in the cardboard box, the box tunnel, the laundry basket, under the blanket on the chair, behind the monstera, in the hallway shoe pile or on the kitchen cart shelf. If Maria sees you go in, she'll come and check. Under the table she has to kneel down first, so run!

⚙️ Settings

🎬 Credits

The room is a 3D rebuild of a real flat, made in Blender from 12 photos, with the posters, apron and window view textured from those photos.

Characters, props, hiding spots and the minestrone were modelled procedurally in Blender. The game runs on three.js. The music and sound effects are synthesised live with the Web Audio API.

No vegetables were harmed. Probably.
